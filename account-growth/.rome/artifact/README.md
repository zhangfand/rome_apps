# Account Growth

Turn live X and LinkedIn signals into a focused, draft-only weekly growth plan.

## What it does

- Reviews your niche, audience, recent posts, and visible performance.
- Drafts five original X posts each week across takes, visuals, build notes, and substantive questions.
- Finds larger relevant accounts and specific conversations where you can contribute genuinely.
- Saves every plan and recommends which topics and formats to repeat, test, or pause.
- Keeps a hard boundary: nothing is ever published or sent without your approval.

Use it when you want a consistent content rhythm without turning your account into generic growth content.
